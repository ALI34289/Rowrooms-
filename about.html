About Laksh Hospital

A legacy of care, a future of health.

Our History

Founded in 2002, Laksh Hospital started as a small clinic with a big heart. Our founder, Dr. Alok Sharma, envisioned a healthcare facility that combined medical excellence with a deep sense of compassion. Over the years, we have grown into a multi-specialty hospital, but our core values remain the same.

Our Journey

2002 - The Beginning

Laksh Hospital was founded as a small 10-bed clinic with a focus on general medicine.

2008 - Expansion

Expanded to a 50-bed facility and added Cardiology and Orthopedics departments.

2015 - New Wing

Inaugurated a new wing with advanced surgical theaters and a dedicated pediatric unit.

2023 - NABH Accreditation

Received NABH accreditation, a testament to our commitment to quality and patient safety.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, affordable, and high-quality healthcare services to our community, fostering an environment of trust and healing.

Our Vision

To be the leading healthcare provider in the region, recognized for our clinical excellence, patient-centric approach, and commitment to innovation.
