Our Services

Comprehensive medical care across a wide range of specialties.

Cardiology

Expert care for your heart. We offer a full range of diagnostic and therapeutic services, including ECG, echocardiography, and stress tests.

Orthopedics

Comprehensive care for bone and joint issues, from fractures and sprains to complex joint replacement surgeries.

Pediatrics

Specialized and compassionate medical care for infants, children, and adolescents, focusing on their unique needs.

Neurology

Diagnosis and treatment of disorders of the nervous system, including stroke, epilepsy, and multiple sclerosis.

Pulmonology

Care for respiratory system diseases, including asthma, COPD, and pneumonia, with advanced diagnostic tools.

Gastroenterology

Treatment for digestive system disorders, including GERD, IBS, and liver diseases, with endoscopic procedures.
